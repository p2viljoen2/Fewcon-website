FEWCON is a forward-thinking consultancy dedicated to sustainable water and environmental solutions, serving clients in the mining, industrial, and agricultural sectors throughout Sub-Saharan Africa. Leveraging our deep expertise and years of experience, we provide practical, customised strategies to address water and environmental challenges while ensuring compliance and minimising risks.

Our mission is to drive sustainable progress through holistic water and environmental management. We aim to lead the industry by offering a seamless, multi-disciplinary platform that delivers innovative solutions for water use licensing, wetland delineation, environmental auditing, and beyond. Through trusted partnerships with our clients, FEWCON ensures exceptional guidance and promotes industry-leading standards.

Services

Water Use Licensing (WULA) and General Authorization

  • Water Use Licensing: Expert assistance in applications under Section 21 of the National Water Act (Act 36 of 1998), DWS risk matrix (SACNASP registered), regulatory authority engagement, and follow-up.
  • General Authorisation: Support for general authorisations related to water use.

Wetland Delineation and Functional Assessments

  • Accurate identification and functional evaluation of wetland areas.
  • Water Balance assessments: Detailed analysis to optimise water usage.
  • Hydropedology Assessments: Studying soil-water relationships for improved management.

Water Use Auditing and Management

  • WULA and GN704 Auditing to ensure continuous compliance for industry and mining operations.

Environmental Compliance and Auditing

  • Environmental Auditing (ECO services): Independent verification of compliance with approvals, licences, and EMPrs.
  • Regulation 34 Compliance Auditing: External auditing for regulatory compliance.

Environmental Planning and Development

  • Environmental Management Programme (EMPr): Comprehensive plans to minimise impacts and ensure compliance with standards.
  • Maintenance Management Plans: Ongoing support for environmental upkeep.

Contaminated Land and Waste Management licensing

  • Contaminated Land Management: Site assessments (Risk Assessment) to identify pathways, receptors, and risks, with reference to NEMA Section 30.
  • Waste Audit, Classification and Management Plans: Review and categorisation of waste streams in line with GN 636 and SANS 10234 standards.
